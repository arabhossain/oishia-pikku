As the Wiki interface is in English by default, if you would like to view it in your preferred language, 
you may right-click on the Wiki page and select "Translate to [language]".
In the newly opened translation window, click the three dots in the top right corner, 
choose "Another language", locate your desired language, and click "Translate" to switch to your preferred language.
